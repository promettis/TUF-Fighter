TUF FIGHTER - custom sounds
===========================

All sounds are generated by the game. To replace one, put a file with the
matching name in this folder (.wav, .ogg, .mp3 or .flac). Anything that is
missing keeps using the built-in sound.

Music (loops):
  music_menu      menus
  music_fight     during a fight

Effects:
  ui_move         menu cursor
  ui_select       menu confirm
  ui_back         menu back
  whoosh_light    light attack swing
  whoosh_heavy    heavy attack swing
  hit_light       light hit
  hit_heavy       heavy hit
  block           blocked attack
  jump            jump
  land            landing
  body_fall       knocked down fighter hits the ground
  special         special move starts
  shot            energy shot fired
  explode         energy shot explodes
  charge          super charging up
  beam            super beam firing
  ko              knock out
  round           round bell
  fight           "FIGHT!" call
  vs              versus screen
  win             round won

Example:  hit_heavy.wav   music_fight.ogg
